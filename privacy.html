This Privacy Policy explains how Summit Crest Capital ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and protects your information when you visit our website (the "Site") or communicate with us.

1. Information We Collect

Information you give us:

  • Contact information, such as your first and last name, phone number, and email address
  • Property information, such as the property address, condition, and details about your situation
  • Anything else you share with us by form, phone, text, or email

Information collected automatically: When you visit the Site, we and our service providers may collect technical data such as your IP address, browser type, device type, pages visited, and referring website, using cookies and similar technologies.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To respond to your property inquiries and prepare cash offers
  • To communicate with you by phone, text message, and email about your property and any transaction
  • To schedule appointments and property walkthroughs
  • To complete purchase transactions, including working with title companies and closing agents
  • To improve the Site and our services
  • To comply with legal obligations and protect our rights

3. SMS Text Messaging

If you consent to receive text messages, we use your mobile number to send messages related to your property inquiry and transaction. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out at any time, or HELP for help.

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with, sold to, or transferred to any third party. Information sharing with subcontractors in support services, such as customer service or messaging platform providers, is permitted only to deliver the messaging service.

4. How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only as needed to serve you:

  • Service providers who help us run our business, such as CRM, phone, messaging, email, and website hosting providers, bound to use your information only to provide services to us
  • Transaction parties such as title companies, escrow and closing agents, attorneys, and, where disclosed in your purchase agreement, an assigned buyer
  • Legal requirements when required by law, subpoena, or court order, or to protect our rights and safety
  • Business transfers in connection with a merger, sale, or reorganization of our business

5. Cookies

We may use cookies and similar tools to understand how visitors use the Site. You can disable cookies in your browser settings, though some features may not work properly.

6. Data Security

We use commercially reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect your information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Data Retention

We keep your information as long as needed to respond to your inquiry, complete any transaction, and meet legal and recordkeeping requirements.

8. Your Rights and Choices

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information by contacting us below. You can opt out of text messages by replying STOP, and out of emails by using the unsubscribe link or replying to ask us to stop. Depending on your state of residence, you may have additional privacy rights under state law.
