Common questions

Questions sellers ask us

How fast can you close on my house?

As little as 7 days. Because we pay cash, there is no loan underwriting or appraisal slowing things down. If you need more time, that is fine too. You pick the date.

Do I need to make repairs before selling?

No. We buy houses as-is. Roof, foundation, plumbing, outdated everything, it does not matter. You do not need to clean or stage either. We handle all of that after closing.

What fees or commissions do you charge?

None. No realtor commissions, no closing costs, no hidden fees. A traditional sale can cost 6 to 10% in commissions and closing expenses. With us, the price we agree on is what you walk away with.

How do you come up with your offer?

We look at the property's condition, location, recent comparable sales nearby, and what it will cost to fix up. You will have your offer within 24 hours of sending us your details, and we are happy to walk you through the numbers.

Will your offer be less than what I could list for?

Usually yes. We take on the repairs, the holding costs, and the risk, and you get speed, certainty, and zero fees in return. Plenty of sellers do better with us once commissions, repairs, and months of payments come out of a listing price.

Do you buy houses in my area?

We buy nationwide, in markets across the United States. Send us the address and we will tell you right away whether we can make an offer on it.

Am I obligated to accept the offer?

Not at all. The offer is free and there is no pressure. If it does not work for you, you can walk away.

Ready to get your cash offer?

Send us the address and we will take it from there. No pressure, no obligation.
